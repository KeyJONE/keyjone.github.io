ZHANG, Qi – Research

Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering, University of Macau

Research overview

My interests connect continuum mechanics, numerical discretization, and learning methods for geomaterials.

Computational Poromechanics

I study the mechanical processes involved in the formation and recovery of earth resources in sedimentary rocks, with an interest in energy and environmental sustainability. Coupled fluid flow and solid deformation are central to this work.

Smoothed Finite Element Method

My numerical-method interests include the smoothed finite element method.

Finite Element Method / Finite Volume Method

I am interested in finite element and finite volume discretizations for complex geomaterials and nonlinear continuum mechanics, including their connection to machine learning.

Neural Network

I am interested in physics-informed neural networks for geomaterials and FEM/FVM discretization-guided machine learning. Related interests include statistical learning for identifying hazards in mining and tunneling.
